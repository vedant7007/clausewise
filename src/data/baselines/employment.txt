FAIR BASELINE: EMPLOYMENT OFFER AND TERMS

This reference text shows balanced terms commonly seen in salaried employment offers. It is for comparison only and is not a template for any particular place.

1. PLACE OF WORK. Your place of work is the city stated in this offer. A permanent transfer to another city requires your written consent, and relocation costs are paid by the Company.

2. PROBATION. Probation lasts up to three (3) months and may be extended once, by up to three (3) months, with written reasons. Either party may end employment during probation with fifteen (15) days' notice.

3. COMPENSATION. Fixed pay is paid monthly. Any performance bonus is paid according to a written bonus policy, and a bonus earned for a completed period is paid even if you are serving notice.

4. JOINING BONUS. If you resign within twelve (12) months of joining, you repay the joining bonus pro-rata for the unserved months, without interest.

5. TRAINING COSTS. Where the Company pays for external certified training worth more than one month's salary, it may agree with you in writing, before the training starts, a repayment that reduces monthly over no more than twelve (12) months and never exceeds the actual cost of that training.

6. WORKING HOURS. Normal working hours are 40 to 48 hours per week. Work beyond these hours is compensated by overtime pay or time off in lieu, as the law and Company policy provide.

7. LEAVE. Paid leave accrues monthly. Unused leave up to a stated limit carries forward, and accrued unused leave is paid out on exit.

8. NOTICE PERIOD. After confirmation, either party may end employment with thirty (30) to sixty (60) days' written notice. You may shorten your notice by paying basic salary for the unserved period, subject to a reasonable handover.

9. TERMINATION FOR MISCONDUCT. Dismissal for misconduct follows a written inquiry in which you are told the allegations and given a fair chance to respond.

10. EXIT DOCUMENTS. Your relieving letter and experience certificate are issued on your last working day, and full and final settlement is paid within thirty (30) days.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY. You keep the Company's confidential information private during employment and for two (2) years afterwards. Information that is public or that you must disclose by law is excluded.

12. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Work you create in the course of your employment, or using Company resources, belongs to the Company. Work you create on your own time, without Company resources and unrelated to the Company's business, remains yours.

13. AFTER YOU LEAVE. For six (6) months after leaving you will not solicit Company clients you personally handled or persuade employees to leave. There is no general restriction on working for other employers.

14. DEDUCTIONS. The Company deducts only amounts required by law or that you have agreed to in writing for a specific item.
