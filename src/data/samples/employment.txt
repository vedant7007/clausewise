We are pleased to offer you the position of Software Engineer II on the following terms. Please read them carefully and sign where indicated.

1. START DATE AND PLACE OF WORK
Your employment will begin on 1 October 2026. Your place of work will be Hyderabad; however, the Company may transfer you to any office, client site or group company in India or abroad at its sole discretion, and refusal to accept a transfer will be treated as voluntary resignation.

2. PROBATION
You will be on probation for six (6) months. The Company may extend probation at its discretion without limit. During probation, the Company may terminate your employment with seven (7) days' notice or pay in lieu.

3. COMPENSATION
3.1 Your fixed annual compensation will be Rs. 18,00,000 (Rupees Eighteen Lakh only), paid monthly in arrears, subject to statutory deductions.
3.2 You are eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 15% of fixed pay. The bonus is entirely discretionary, and no bonus shall be payable if you are serving notice or are not on the rolls on the date of payout.
3.3 Salary details will be credited to your salary account after you submit your PAN (format: ABCDE1234F) and bank details to HR.

4. JOINING BONUS AND CLAWBACK
You will receive a one-time joining bonus of Rs. 2,00,000. If you leave the Company for any reason within twenty-four (24) months of joining, you must repay the full joining bonus, together with interest at 18% per annum, within seven (7) days of your last working day.

5. TRAINING BOND
The Company may nominate you for training programmes. You agree to remain employed for at least two (2) years after the Company first nominates you for any training. If you resign or are terminated for any reason before then, you shall pay the Company liquidated damages of Rs. 3,00,000, which you agree is a genuine pre-estimate of the Company's loss.

6. WORKING HOURS
Normal working hours are 45 hours per week. You may be required to work additional hours, weekends and public holidays as business needs require, without additional compensation.

7. LEAVE
You are entitled to 18 days of paid leave per calendar year, pro-rated for the first year. Unused leave lapses at the end of each year and is not encashed on exit.

8. NOTICE PERIOD AND TERMINATION
8.1 After confirmation, either party may terminate employment by giving ninety (90) days' written notice. The Company may, at its option, pay salary in lieu of notice. You may not buy out your notice period, and the Company may refuse to relieve you early.
8.2 The Company may terminate your employment immediately and without notice for misconduct, which includes any act the Company considers detrimental to its interests.
8.3 The relieving letter and experience certificate will be issued only after full and final settlement, which may take up to ninety (90) days after your last working day.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY
You shall keep confidential all non-public information about the Company, its clients and its employees, during your employment and for five (5) years afterwards. This obligation does not apply to information that is publicly available or that you are required by law to disclose.

10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work, inventions, code and ideas that you create during your employment, whether or not during working hours and whether or not using Company resources, shall belong exclusively to the Company.

11. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION
11.1 For twenty-four (24) months after leaving the Company, you shall not work for, consult for, or start any business that competes with the Company anywhere in the world.
11.2 For twelve (12) months after leaving, you shall not solicit any employee or client of the Company.

12. DEDUCTIONS
The Company may deduct from your salary or final settlement any amount you owe the Company, including any amount the Company determines to be a loss caused by you.

13. GOVERNING LAW
This offer is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Hyderabad shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

14. ACCEPTANCE
Please sign and return a copy of this letter by 20 August 2026. If we do not receive your acceptance by then, this offer will lapse.
