FAIR BASELINE: RESIDENTIAL LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This reference text shows balanced terms commonly seen in residential licences. It is for comparison only and is not a template for any particular place.

1. TERM. The licence runs for eleven (11) months from the start date stated in this Agreement.

2. LICENCE FEE. The Licensee pays the agreed monthly licence fee on or before the 5th day of each month. If payment is more than seven (7) days late, a late fee of 1% of the monthly fee per week of delay applies, capped at 10% of one month's fee.

3. FEE REVISION. The licence fee is fixed for the term. Any increase applies only on renewal and must be agreed in writing by both parties.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. The Licensee pays a refundable security deposit equal to two (2) months' licence fee. The Licensor returns the deposit within thirty (30) days of the Licensee vacating, less only unpaid dues and the reasonable cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear, with an itemised statement and receipts for every deduction.

5. REPAIRS. The Licensee pays for minor day-to-day repairs and damage caused by the Licensee. The Licensor pays for structural repairs, major plumbing and electrical faults, and repair or replacement of appliances supplied by the Licensor that fail through normal use.

6. ACCESS. The Licensor may enter the Premises for inspection or repairs at reasonable times after giving at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice, except in an emergency.

7. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving one (1) month's written notice. Any lock-in period applies equally to both parties, and early exit during a lock-in costs no more than one month's licence fee.

8. HOLDING OVER. If the Licensee stays after the Agreement ends, the Licensee pays a pro-rated licence fee for the extra days.

9. RENEWAL. The Agreement may be renewed only by a new written agreement signed by both parties. It does not renew automatically.

10. LIABILITY. Each party is responsible for losses caused by its own negligence or breach. The Licensor remains responsible for injury or damage caused by the Licensor's negligence or by defects in the Premises the Licensor knew about.

11. NOTICES. Notices may be sent by either party by email or registered post to the addresses in this Agreement, and are treated as received two (2) days after sending.

12. DISPUTES. The parties will try to resolve disputes through discussion first. Any arbitrator must be jointly appointed, and each party bears its own costs unless the arbitrator decides otherwise.

13. COSTS. Stamp duty and registration charges are shared equally by the parties.
